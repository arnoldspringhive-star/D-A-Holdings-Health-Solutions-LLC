Compassionate, individualized, and evidence-informed behavioral care and recovery programs designed to support emotional wellness, stability, and sustainable life change.

WHAT CLIENTS CAN EXPECT

Compassionate Support for Sustainable Recovery

We understand that substance use challenges can affect every area of life, including relationships, family life, employment, physical health, and emotional well-being. At D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC, our approach begins by listening.

We take the time to understand each client's unique concerns, symptoms, personal strengths, circumstances, and recovery goals before developing an individualized plan of care. Our philosophy is centered on treating the whole person with dignity, respect, and confidentiality.

SAFE & CONFIDENTIAL

CLINICAL OFFERINGS

Substance Abuse Services & Programs Offered

We provide structured outpatient services, comprehensive diagnostic assessments, dual-diagnosis treatment, and rehabilitation tailored to each client's specific clinical needs.

01 • CLINICAL ASSESSMENT

Substance Use Evaluations

Comprehensive evaluations reviewing current symptoms, psychiatric and medical history, and substance use when clinically relevant to guide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, and the development of an individualized care plan.

Get Started
02 • STRUCTURED CARE

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Structured, evidence-based outpatient treatment designed for individuals who require focused clinical support and structured recovery routines while continuing to maintain work, education, and family responsibilities.

Learn More
03 • DAILY SUPPORT

Partial Hospitalization (PHP)

Comprehensive daily therapeutic support and clinical monitoring offering intensive intervention, coping strategy development, and symptom stabilization without requiring an overnight inpatient hospital stay.

Learn More
04 • PREVENTION & SKILLS

Relapse Prevention & Education

Equipping clients and families with practical information regarding triggers, symptom awareness, healthy daily routines, stress management, and actionable relapse-prevention strategies for long-term recovery.

Learn More
05 • DUAL DIAGNOSIS

Co-Occurring Mental Health Care

Integrated psychiatric evaluation and medication management for underlying or co-occurring mental health conditions—such as depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, and trauma—that often accompany substance use.

View Conditions
06 • REHABILITATION

Psychiatric Rehabilitation (PRP)

Supportive rehabilitation focused on practical life skills, independence, community reintegration, and emotional stability to foster long-term personal resilience and improved daily functioning.

Get Started
