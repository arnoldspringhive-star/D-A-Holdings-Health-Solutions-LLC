Answers About Our Services & Policies

If you have questions not covered below, please feel free to reach out to our team at 240-917-6203.

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC provides mental health services to children and adolescents ages 6–17 and adults ages 18–67.

We evaluate and treat a variety of psychiatric and behavioral conditions, including ADHD, depression, anxiety disorders, ODD, conduct disorder, DMDD, bipolar and related disorders, borderline personality disorder, schizophrenia, other psychotic disorders, and other mood or behavioral concerns within the scope of our services.

Yes. We provide comprehensive psychiatric evaluations to assess symptoms, history, functioning, and treatment needs.

When clinically appropriate, qualified psychiatric providers may prescribe medication and provide ongoing medication-management services.

No. Medication recommendations are based on the results of your psychiatric evaluation, clinical needs, medical considerations, and other relevant factors. An evaluation does not guarantee that we will prescribe a particular medication.

We encourage you to discuss previous medications, treatment experiences, and concerns with your provider. However, we make prescribing decisions based on clinical assessment, safety considerations, professional judgment, and appropriate standards of care.

The frequency of follow-up appointments depends on your symptoms, diagnosis, medications, response to treatment, and individual clinical needs. Your provider will recommend an appropriate follow-up schedule.

Yes. We serve children and adolescents ages 6–17. Parents or legal guardians are involved in treatment as clinically and legally appropriate.

We take patient privacy seriously. We handle health information in accordance with applicable privacy and confidentiality requirements. In certain circumstances, law may require or permit disclosure, which we can explain as part of the informed-consent process.

Yes. Telehealth services may be available when clinically appropriate and permitted by applicable laws and regulations. These services are conducted via a private and secure platform.

Website Emergency & Crisis Disclaimer

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate medical or psychiatric emergency, is in immediate danger, or is at risk of harming themselves or another person, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

For mental health, suicide, or substance-use crisis support in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
