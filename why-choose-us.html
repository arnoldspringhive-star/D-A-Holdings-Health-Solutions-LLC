TRUSTED PARTNERS IN CARE

A Welcoming, Respectful, and Supportive Space

Finding the right mental health provider is an important part of your journey toward emotional wellness. At D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC, we strive to provide a welcoming, respectful, and supportive environment where children, adolescents, adults, and families feel heard and valued.

We recognize that every individual has different experiences, challenges, strengths, and treatment goals. Our approach is centered on understanding the whole person and developing an individualized treatment plan based on each client's unique clinical needs.

OUR CORE ADVANTAGES

Why Patients and Families Choose Us

The core foundations that define our commitment to quality psychiatric and behavioral health care:

1

Individualized Care

We recognize that mental health treatment is not one-size-fits-all. We tailor treatment recommendations to each client's symptoms, history, developmental needs, strengths, preferences, and goals.

2

Care Across the Lifespan

We provide behavioral and mental health services for children and adolescents ages 6–17 and adults ages 18–67, meeting clients at every milestone.

3

Comprehensive Psychiatric Services

Our services include psychiatric evaluations, diagnostic assessments, medication management, ongoing psychiatric monitoring, patient education, and care coordination.

4

Family-Centered Approach

For children and adolescents, we recognize the essential role parents and guardians play in treatment. When appropriate, we collaborate with families and other professionals involved in the child's care.

5

Compassionate Environment

We strive to create a safe, respectful, confidential, and nonjudgmental environment where clients can openly discuss their concerns and focus on wellness.

6

Collaborative Treatment

We encourage clients to participate actively in treatment decisions. When clinically appropriate, we also collaborate with therapists, primary care providers, schools, and specialists.

7

Ongoing Support Across Your Journey

Mental health treatment is often a journey, not a single appointment. We provide ongoing monitoring to evaluate symptoms, treatment response, medication effectiveness, potential side effects, and progress toward treatment goals.

Website Emergency & Crisis Disclaimer

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate medical or psychiatric emergency, is in immediate danger, or is at risk of harming themselves or another person, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

For mental health, suicide, or substance-use crisis support in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
