Adult Mental Health Services (Ages 18–67)

Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation and individualized treatment for adults experiencing mood, anxiety, attention, personality, or psychotic disorders. Focused on emotional stability, functioning, and quality of life.

Ages 18–67 Individualized Care Medication Management
SUPPORTING ADULT WELLNESS

Restoring Balance, Functioning & Independence

Mental health challenges can affect relationships, employment, physical health, independence, and overall quality of life.

Our adult psychiatric services focus on understanding each client's symptoms and how those symptoms affect daily functioning. We work collaboratively with clients to establish realistic treatment goals and develop individualized treatment recommendations.

Whether an individual is experiencing anxiety, depression, attention difficulties, mood instability, psychotic symptoms, or another psychiatric concern, our goal is to provide compassionate and respectful care while promoting stability and improved functioning.

Conditions We Treat in Adults (Ages 18–67)

We provide psychiatric evaluation and evidence-informed care for a broad range of mental and emotional health concerns:

✓

Depression & Depressive Disorders

Addressing low mood, lack of energy, changes in appetite or sleep, and loss of interest in daily life.

✓

Anxiety Disorders

✓

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

Psychiatric care, medication monitoring, and relapse prevention for individuals experiencing psychotic conditions.

IN-DEPTH CARE

Mood Disorders

Mood disorders can significantly affect a person's emotions, energy, sleep, concentration, relationships, and ability to function in everyday life.

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC provides assessment, treatment, education, and ongoing psychiatric care for individuals experiencing mood-related conditions, including depressive disorders and bipolar and related disorders.

Treatment recommendations may include medication management, psychotherapy referrals, lifestyle recommendations, and coordination with other healthcare professionals.

SPECIALIZED SUPPORT

Schizophrenia & Psychotic Disorders

We provide psychiatric care for individuals experiencing schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders. Symptoms may include hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thinking or behavior, social withdrawal, and changes in functioning.

Our approach emphasizes careful assessment, medication management when appropriate, ongoing symptom monitoring, education, treatment adherence, relapse prevention, and collaboration with family members and other healthcare professionals when appropriate.
