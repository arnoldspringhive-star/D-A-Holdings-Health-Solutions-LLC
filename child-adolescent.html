Child & Adolescent Mental Health Services

Specialized psychiatric evaluation and evidence-informed care for children and adolescents ages 6–17. Partnering with parents, guardians, and educators to support emotional stability and healthy development.

Ages 6–17 Family-Centered Care ADHD & Behavioral Support
UNDERSTANDING YOUNG MINDS

Support for Emotional & Behavioral Challenges

Children and adolescents may experience emotional and behavioral challenges that interfere with their development, education, friendships, relationships, and family life.

Children and adolescents experience mental health challenges differently from adults. Their emotional and behavioral needs may also be influenced by developmental stage, family relationships, school experiences, peer relationships, and their environment.

We recognize the important role that parents and guardians play in a child's treatment and strive to collaborate with families while respecting each child's developmental needs.

EVALUATION & TREATMENT

Conditions We Evaluate & Treat (Ages 6–17)

We provide thorough psychiatric assessment and tailored treatment planning for a wide variety of childhood and adolescent concerns:

✓

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

Evaluation and treatment recommendations addressing attention, organization, restlessness, and impulse control.

✓

Anxiety Disorders

Support for excessive worry, nervousness, social anxiety, separation distress, and school-related stress.

✓

Depressive Disorders

Care for persistent low mood, withdrawal, loss of interest, irritability, and changes in energy or sleep.

✓

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

Assessment and interventions for intrusive thoughts, recurring compulsions, and ritualized behaviors.

✓

Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD)

Care for persistent patterns of irritability, defiance, argumentativeness, and difficulty complying with expectations.

✓

Conduct Disorder

Comprehensive assessment for persistent behavioral challenges interfering with functioning at home or school.

✓

Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder (DMDD)

Care for persistent irritability and significant emotional or behavioral outbursts in children and teens.

✓

Mood & Emotional Regulation Difficulties

Support for youth struggling to process intense emotions, rapid mood shifts, and coping with stress.

FAMILY-CENTERED CARE

Partnering with Parents, Guardians & Schools.

At D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC, we strive to partner with parents and guardians to better understand the child's needs and identify appropriate interventions.

When clinically appropriate and with proper authorization, coordination may occur with therapists, pediatricians, primary care providers, schools, and other professionals involved in the child's care.

Our goal is to support healthier emotional development, improved behavior, stronger coping skills, better functioning, and healthier family relationships.
