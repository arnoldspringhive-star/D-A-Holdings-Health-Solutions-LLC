About Us

Dedicated to providing compassionate, individualized, and evidence-informed behavioral and mental health services for children, adolescents, and adults.

Ages 6–17 & 18–67 Waldorf, MD Whole-Person Care
OUR PRACTICE

Compassionate, Patient-Centered Psychiatric Care.

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is a healthcare organization dedicated to providing compassionate, individualized, and evidence-informed behavioral and mental health services.

We serve children and adolescents between the ages of 6 and 17 and adults between the ages of 18 and 67 who may be experiencing emotional, behavioral, mood, or psychiatric difficulties.

We recognize that every person's experience is unique. For this reason, our approach begins with listening. We take the time to understand each client's concerns, symptoms, strengths, personal circumstances, and treatment goals before developing an individualized plan of care.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Treating the whole person rather than solely a diagnosis.

Our philosophy is centered on treating the whole person rather than focusing solely on a diagnosis. We strive to create a therapeutic environment built on dignity, trust, collaboration, cultural sensitivity, and respect.

Our goal is to help individuals achieve greater emotional stability, strengthen coping skills, improve daily functioning, and enhance their overall quality of life. Through ongoing assessment, education, treatment, and support, we work collaboratively with our clients and, when appropriate, their families and other healthcare professionals to promote continuity of care.

At D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC, we believe quality mental healthcare should empower individuals to better understand their needs, recognize their strengths, and actively participate in decisions about their treatment and wellness.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Our mission at D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is to provide accessible, compassionate, individualized, and high-quality mental health services that empower children, adolescents, adults, and families to achieve greater emotional stability, improved functioning, and a better quality of life.

OUR ASPIRATION

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a community where individuals and families feel supported in seeking mental health care and have access to respectful, comprehensive, and person-centered services that promote hope, resilience, recovery, and long-term wellness.

CLINICAL EXCELLENCE

Our Approach to Care

At D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC, we believe effective mental health treatment begins with understanding the whole person—not simply treating a diagnosis. Our approach emphasizes:

01

Compassionate & Respectful Care

Treating every client with dignity, compassion, respect, and confidentiality in every interaction.

02

Comprehensive Psychiatric Assessment

Thorough evaluation of current symptoms, medical history, family context, and daily functioning.

03

Individualized Treatment Planning

Customized plans tailored to developmental stage, personal strengths, clinical needs, and goals.

04

Evidence-Informed Treatment

Utilizing clinically sound and scientifically supported psychiatric modalities to promote wellness.

05

Medication Education & Monitoring

Thoughtful prescribing with continuous monitoring for effectiveness, safety, and client understanding.

06

Collaboration with Clients & Families

Encouraging active partnership and family involvement, especially for child and adolescent clients.

07

Care Coordination

Coordination with therapists, pediatricians, primary care, schools, and specialists when appropriate.

08

Ongoing Evaluation of Progress

Regular review and adjustment of treatment plans based on response, stability, and personal milestones.

09

Confidentiality & Dignity

Strict adherence to health privacy standards and steadfast protection of patient confidentiality.

"Our goal is to establish a therapeutic partnership that empowers clients and families to actively participate in treatment and make informed decisions about their mental health care."

Website Emergency & Crisis Disclaimer

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate medical or psychiatric emergency, is in immediate danger, or is at risk of harming themselves or another person, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

For mental health, suicide, or substance-use crisis support in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

* Do not use website contact forms, email, voicemail, or routine appointment requests for emergency situations.
