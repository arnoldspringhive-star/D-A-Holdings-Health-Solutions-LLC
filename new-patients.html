WELCOME NEW PATIENTS

Your First Psychiatric Appointment

Thank you for considering D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC for your mental health care.

Your initial psychiatric evaluation gives your provider an opportunity to learn about your concerns and recommend appropriate treatment. Following the assessment, your provider will discuss clinical impressions and treatment recommendations with you.

Parents or legal guardians should participate as appropriately when services are provided to minors.

WHAT TO EXPECT

During Your Initial Appointment

During your initial evaluation, your provider will conduct a thorough review to understand your complete clinical picture:

1

Current Symptoms & Concerns

What prompted your visit and your immediate challenges.

2

Psychiatric History

Previous diagnoses, treatment experiences, and outcomes.

3

Medical History

Current and chronic physical health conditions.

4

Current & Previous Medications

All prescription and non-prescription regimens.

5

Medication Allergies

Known allergies and previous adverse reactions.

6

Family Psychiatric & Medical History

Hereditary factors and family health backgrounds.

7

Social & Developmental History

Milestones, environment, relationships, and upbringing.

8

School or Employment Functioning

Performance, attendance, social interactions, and focus.

9

Sleep and Appetite

Daily biological rhythms and physical energy levels.

10

Substance Use History

Reviewed when clinically relevant for safety and care.

11

Previous Therapy or Treatment

Counseling modalities and past care responsiveness.

12

Current Stressors & Support Systems

Environmental factors, family support, and community ties.

13

Safety Concerns

Evaluation of acute distress, risks, or self-harm concerns.

14

Your Personal Goals for Treatment

What you hope to achieve and prioritize on your healing journey.

APPOINTMENT CHECKLIST

Preparing for Your Appointment

To help us provide a comprehensive assessment, please have the following information available when applicable:

✓

Current Medication List

Include exact names, dosages, and prescribing schedules.

✓

Medication Allergy Information

Details of known allergies or previous negative drug reactions.

✓

Relevant Medical & Psychiatric Records

Past records, assessments, or treatment notes.

✓

Previous Psychiatric Medication History

Past medications tried, effectiveness, and reasons for stopping.

✓

Recent Laboratory Results

Relevant blood work or medical labs when requested.

✓

Hospital / Emergency Discharge Records

Recent inpatient or emergency summaries, if applicable.

✓

Contact Info for Treating Providers

Primary care physician, therapist, or previous specialist.

✓

School Evaluations (For Children)

IEP, 504 plans, or behavioral evaluations when available.

✓

Insurance & Identification Information

Photo ID and insurance cards as required by the practice.

📝 Please complete requested intake forms before your appointment whenever possible.

Website Emergency & Crisis Disclaimer

D & A Holdings Health Solutions LLC is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate medical or psychiatric emergency, is in immediate danger, or is at risk of harming themselves or another person, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

For mental health, suicide, or substance-use crisis support in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
